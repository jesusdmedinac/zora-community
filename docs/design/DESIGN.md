# Zora M3 Design System: The Automotive Intelligence Framework

## Brand Identity & Foundations
- **Vibe**: High-precision automotive intelligence, dark industrial titanium, local-first privacy security, zero fluff.
- **Base Canvas**: Deep Obsidian Black `#0D0E11`
- **Surface Elevation (Cards/Sheets)**: Dark Titanium Surface `#12151C` with hairline border `rgba(74, 68, 85, 0.3)`.
- **Primary Brand Gradient**: Electric Violet `#894CFF` through Neon Purple `#A833FD` to Vibrant Rose `#EC168A`.
- **Functional Semantics**:
  - Direct Fit / Optimal Level: Emerald `#10B981`
  - Maintenance Warning / Low Level: Amber `#F59E0B`
  - Critical / Fault Alert: Crimson `#EF4444`

## Typography Strategy
- **Headlines & Display**: Manrope (Bold, clean, geometric)
- **Body & UI**: Inter (Crisp legibility)
- **Technical Telemetry & OEM Numbers**: JetBrains Mono (Engine codes, VIN, specs)

## Shapes & Radii
- **Cards and Containers**: 12px to 16px rounded corners (`rounded-xl` / `rounded-2xl`)
- **Interactive Pills, CTA and Chips**: Fully rounded capsules (`rounded-full` / `9999px`)
